01 — The big picture

One region. A changing climate.

Asia and the Pacific is home to billions of people and some of the world's most diverse landscapes—from major cities and fertile river valleys to tropical forests and small island nations.

As greenhouse gases build up in the atmosphere, the planet warms. This affects weather patterns, oceans and ecosystems. Communities across the region are already experiencing the consequences, although the risks differ from place to place.

Climate change is an environmental, social and economic challenge. It affects the places people live, the food they grow, the water they use and the livelihoods they depend on.

02 — What is changing?

The impacts are all around us.

Climate change can affect natural systems and everyday life. Here are six important examples across Asia and the Pacific.

☀️

Rising temperatures

Hotter days and heatwaves can affect health, schools, outdoor workers, crops and electricity demand.

🌧️

Floods and heavy rain

Intense rainfall can cause flooding and landslides, damaging homes, roads, farms and public services.

🌊

Sea-level rise

Rising seas increase risks for low-lying islands and coastal communities through flooding and erosion.

🌾

Food and water security

Changing rainfall, drought and heat can put pressure on agriculture, fisheries and freshwater supplies.

🐠

Ocean and ecosystem stress

Warmer oceans and marine heatwaves can damage coral reefs and affect marine life and fishing communities.

🏔️

Glacier loss

Retreating glaciers in high mountain Asia can change river flows and create risks for downstream communities.

03 — Regional perspectives

Different places, connected challenges.

Geography, resources and local infrastructure shape how climate change is experienced.

🇵🇭

Philippines & Southeast Asia

Coastal communities and islands face risks from sea-level rise, heavy rainfall, tropical cyclones and extreme heat. Mangroves and healthy watersheds can help protect communities.

🏝️

Pacific Islands

Low-lying islands face serious challenges from rising seas, coastal erosion, saltwater intrusion and threats to freshwater resources.

🏔️

South & Central Asia

Heatwaves, changing monsoon rainfall, drought and glacier retreat can affect water supplies, farming and mountain communities.

🌆

East Asia

Large urban populations and coastal infrastructure must prepare for extreme heat, heavy rainfall and warming oceans.

04 — A path forward

The solutions are within reach.

We need to reduce the pollution driving climate change and prepare for impacts already being experienced. This work is called mitigation and adaptation.

01 / ENERGY

Choose cleaner power

Expand renewable energy, improve efficiency and reduce dependence on fossil fuels.

02 / NATURE

Restore ecosystems

Protect forests, mangroves, wetlands and coral reefs that support people and wildlife.

03 / PREPAREDNESS

Build safer communities

Improve early-warning systems, disaster planning and climate-resilient infrastructure.

04 / DAILY LIFE

Make thoughtful choices

Save energy and water, reduce waste and choose lower-emission travel when possible.

05 / FOOD

Support resilient food systems

Encourage sustainable farming and fishing that can adapt to changing conditions.

06 / COMMUNITY

Learn and work together

Share reliable information and support fair, science-based climate action.
